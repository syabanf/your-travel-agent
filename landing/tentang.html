Our Story

It started at one desk in Medan

Icon Holiday began as a small office in Medan that only issued flight tickets. Requests grew on their own: after tickets, guests asked about hotels; after hotels, about visas; then about sensible routes for three-generation families. We added those services one at a time, because our guests needed them.

Today we operate through two companies, PT. Inter Continent Indonesia in Medan and PT. Icon Holiday Indonesia in Jakarta, with five connected services: tour packages, flight tickets, visa services, hotel bookings and cruises. The goal is to handle each trip in one place, with the same team from the first consultation until you get back home.

We care most about the things a brochure doesn't show: an unhurried pace, hotels close to each day's plans, prices stated plainly from the start, and a phone that gets answered when something goes wrong on the road.

  • An experienced tour leader on every group departure
  • Visa files checked before they are submitted
  • Transparent prices with no hidden fees
  • The same team from consultation to your return
