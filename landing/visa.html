Preparation

Documents most embassies ask for

The list below covers what nearly every embassy asks for. The details still differ by country and by visa type.

Basic documents

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from the departure date
  • Recent passport photo in the size and background the embassy requires
  • Proof of funds: bank statements for the last 3 months and/or a sponsor letter
  • Proof of employment or business: an employment letter, business license (SIUP) or university letter
  • Travel itinerary and return flight tickets
  • Proof of accommodation: hotel vouchers or an invitation letter from your host

Visa requirements change at any time, sometimes without notice. Don't prepare your file from an old list. Talk to us before you gather documents, so nothing goes to waste.
